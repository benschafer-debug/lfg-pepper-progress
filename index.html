Adoption by division

Customers placing orders on Pepper each month, and an estimate of the share of each division's invoiced revenue that was ordered on Pepper.

Why "estimate"? We compare two separate systems: what customers ordered on Pepper, and what the distributor's ERP later invoiced. Orders and invoices are not matched one to one, so this is a close approximation, not an exact figure. Ordered amounts can differ from delivered amounts (substitutions, shorts, credits), and orders placed late in a month are often invoiced the next month. Use it to see direction and trend.
